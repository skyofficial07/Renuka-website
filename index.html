About Renuka

Culture, Voice, and Heritage

Renuka Khatiwada is a professional Nepali cultural singer known for her soulful interpretations of folk songs, devotional music, and cultural classics. Her vocal presence honors the traditions of Nepal while connecting with modern listeners.

From stage performances to digital releases, Renuka celebrates Nepali identity through music, storytelling, and melodic expression.

What makes her unique

  • Authentic Nepali folk melodies
  • Professional stage performances and recordings
  • Authoritative YouTube channel with songs and live videos
